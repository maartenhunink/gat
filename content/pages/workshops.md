Our workshops are immersive experiences designed to help you experience the Gat Method. Each workshop usually consists of a minimum of 3 days, which are taught by one or more of us. During these intensive sessions, participants engage in practical exercises, group discussions, and receive personalized feedback to deepen their understanding and application of the method.
