# The Gat Method is a collection of choreographic tools and practices that have been developed through the last 20 years, in the studio, on stage and in life. It was started by [Emanuel Gat](https://emanuelgatdance.com/biography) and it continues through [all of us](/pages/people). You can join or book one of our [workshops](/pages/workshops).
